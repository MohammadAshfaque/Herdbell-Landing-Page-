Context Bridge

Hit a usage limit?
Keep going in another agent.

Herdbell reads your local session transcript and project files and builds a structured continuation prompt. Paste it into another agent and keep working without explaining the whole project again.

  1. 1Open Herdbell and select your session.
  2. 2Click Generate Context.
  3. 3Click Copy Context and paste it into the next agent.

Runs fully on your Mac, with no AI API calls. How complete the output is depends on what your session transcript and project files contain.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Do I need to log in to my AI accounts or add API keys?

No. Herdbell connects to the agents already installed on your Mac through their supported local hooks. It never asks for credentials or API keys.

Which agents are supported?

Claude Code, OpenAI Codex, Cursor, and Google Antigravity at launch. More integrations are planned.

Will Herdbell mess with my agent configurations?

No. Herdbell keeps your existing settings and backs them up before adding its hooks. You can disconnect an agent at any time.

How does Context Bridge work without an AI model?

It pulls the goal, completed work, decisions, issues, and next steps out of your local session transcript and project files, then formats them as a continuation prompt. How complete the prompt is depends on what that data contains.

Does any of my data leave my Mac?

No. Session data stays local. Herdbell uses the network only to validate your license and check for updates.

Is it a subscription?

No. You pay once and own it, with a 7-day free trial first.
